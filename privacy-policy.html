Chotter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version. Chotter is a family app, so it holds information about children. Parents create the family and decide what goes in it. Children never create accounts and are never asked for an email address. We use what you give us to run the app and nothing else. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking tools in Chotter, and we never sell data. If you ask a child to add a photo to prove a chore, it's deleted as soon as a parent approves or rejects it. You can ask us to delete your family's data at any time.

1. Who we are

Chotter is made by Look Behind You Software Ltd ("LBY Software", "we", "us"), a small company in Scotland. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy under UK data protection law. You can contact us at hello@lbysoftware.co.uk.

2. Who uses Chotter

  • Parents and carers (aged 18 or over) create an account, set up the family and manage everything.
  • Children use Chotter on a device a parent has connected to their profile. Children don't sign up, don't have an email address with us and can't create or join a family on their own.
  • Helpers (for example a grandparent or nanny) can join with a one-time code from a parent. They give a name, don't need a Chotter account, and are only let in once a parent or owner has approved them.

3. What we collect, and why

InformationWhat it is and why we need it
Parent accountYour email address and a password (stored by our authentication provider as a scrambled hash, never in plain text). If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address they share with us, which may be a private relay address in Apple's case. We use this to let you sign in and recover your account.
Family detailsThe family name you choose, who belongs to it and what role each person has (owner, parent or helper). Helpers who join without an account give us a name.
Children's profilesA first name or nickname, a cartoon avatar, and optionally a date of birth and a few interests a parent picks from a list. These are optional and only used to suggest suitable chores and rewards. We recommend using a first name or nickname, not a full name.
Chores, points and rewardsThe chores you create, who they're assigned to, when they're done or approved, points earned and spent, rewards you add or choose, chore suggestions children make, and stickers earned. This is the core of the app.
Reward requestsWhen a child suggests a reward, we store its name and, if they add them, a note and a link to a product or picture. A parent then approves it (and sets the points cost) or declines it. Please encourage children not to put personal details in a request.
Photo proof (optional)If a parent sets a chore to need a photo, the child can take or pick one when they finish it. It is reduced in size on the phone, stored privately, and deleted as soon as a parent approves or rejects the chore. Only members of that family can see it.
Connected devicesWhen a parent connects a child's device with a QR code, that device gets an anonymous sign-in tied to one child profile. We store a device name, the date it was connected and whether it has been disconnected. No email, phone number or advertising ID.
Parent PIN (optional)A 4-digit PIN that stops a child leaving Kid Mode on a shared device. We only store a scrambled hash of it.
Purchase statusWhether your family has bought Family Unlock, and when the free trial ends. Payment itself is handled by Apple or Google (see section 8).
Technical logsOur hosting providers keep short-lived technical logs, which include IP addresses, to keep the service secure and working.

We don't ask for children's surnames, addresses, phone numbers, locations or contacts, and Chotter doesn't track your location.

4. What stays on the device

Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage so you don't have to log in every time. Some settings and cached screens are also kept on the device so the app feels quick. Uninstalling the app removes them.

5. Camera and photos

Chotter asks for camera access for two things only: scanning the QR code that connects a child's device to your family, and taking a proof photo for a chore. It asks for photo library access only so a photo can be attached to a chore. You can refuse either and keep using the rest of the app. Chotter does not record audio or video.

6. Adverts, analytics and tracking

Chotter has no adverts, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or behavioural tracking tools, and no social media trackers. We don't build profiles of you or your children, and we never sell or rent personal data.

7. Who we share data with

We only use the service providers we need to run Chotter. They process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage, on servers in Ireland (within the EEA).
  • RevenueCat helps us manage the Family Unlock purchase. It receives an identifier for your family and the store's purchase record, not your name or email.
  • Apple and Google run the app stores, take payments and provide Sign in with Apple and Google. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do.

Family information is visible to the people in that family, according to their role. A child sees their own profile, chores and rewards. We'll only disclose data to anyone else if the law requires it.

8. Purchases

Chotter has a 15-day free trial, then a one-off in-app purchase called Family Unlock. Payments are taken by Apple or Google. We never see or store your card details. We receive confirmation that the purchase happened, which we store against your family so every adult in it gets the benefit, and so your purchase can be restored if you reinstall the app or change phone.

9. How long we keep data

  • Family, child and chore data is kept for as long as your family uses Chotter, so points, rewards and history stay intact.
  • Proof photos are deleted as soon as a parent approves or rejects them.
  • Invite and connection codes expire or stop working once used.
  • When you ask us to delete your family, we remove its data, normally within 30 days. We may keep a minimal record of the purchase and of your request where the law requires it. Technical logs are removed after a short period.

10. Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law, we process the information above because it's necessary to provide the service you've asked for (a contract with the parent who creates the account) and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working. Optional details, such as a child's date of birth, interests and photos, are provided at the parent's choice and can be removed at any time. We rely on the parent or carer who sets up the family to act on behalf of their children.

11. Children's privacy

Chotter is designed to be used by children with a parent's involvement, and we follow the principles of the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code):

  • Parents control the family, the children's profiles, the chores and the rewards.
  • Children don't create accounts and we don't ask them for an email address, phone number or full name.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking, and no way for children to talk to strangers or share anything outside their family.
  • We collect as little as we can, and optional details are clearly marked as optional.

If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's knowledge, please email us and we'll delete it.

12. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to each family's data is restricted to that family at database level, photos are stored privately and accessed with short-lived links, and PINs and passwords are stored as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and we review our security regularly.

13. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or object to our use of it. A parent can exercise these rights for their children. To make a request, email hello@lbysoftware.co.uk from the address on your Chotter account so we can check it's you. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

14. International transfers

Our main servers are in Ireland. Some of our providers, such as Apple, Google and RevenueCat, may process limited data outside the UK and EEA. Where that happens, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or addendum, or an adequacy decision.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change how Chotter uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell parents in the app if the change is significant.
