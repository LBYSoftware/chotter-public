# Chotter public page

The public page, privacy policy and terms of service for the Chotter app, hosted with GitHub Pages at
https://lbysoftware.github.io/chotter-public/ (served on https://lbysoftware.co.uk/chotter-public/).

- `index.html`: about the app, how it works, pricing, contact
- `privacy-policy.html`: privacy policy (use this link in App Store Connect, Google Play and the Google/Apple sign-in consent screens)
- `terms-of-service.html`: terms of service
- `style.css`: shared styling (Chotter cream, yellow and teal, with dark mode)
