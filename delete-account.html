Delete your Chotter account

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Chotter is made by Look Behind You Software. You can delete your account and your family's data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: delete it in the app

  1. Open Chotter and sign in.
  2. Go to the Family tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, type the confirmation word and tap Delete my account.

Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't get into the app, email hello@lbysoftware.co.uk from the email address on your Chotter account so we can check it's you. Put "Delete my Chotter account" in the subject line. We'll delete your data, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

If you own the family (the person who created it), your account is deleted together with everything in the family:

  • your sign-in details and email address
  • children's profiles: first names or nicknames, avatars, and any optional date of birth or interests
  • chores, Chotter points, rewards, reward requests and stickers
  • chore proof photos
  • paired kid devices
  • your optional parent PIN

Other adults in the family lose access to it. Their own accounts stay.

If you are a second parent, carer or helper, your account and sign-in details are deleted and you leave the family. Chores and rewards you created stay with the family but are no longer linked to you.

What is kept

  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies. The Family Unlock purchase belongs to the family, so it goes with the family in Chotter.
  • A minimal record of the purchase and of your deletion request may be kept where the law requires it.
  • Technical logs are kept for a short period and then removed.

Just want a break?

You can sign out instead. Your family will be there when you come back.

Questions about your data? Email hello@lbysoftware.co.uk. Our Privacy Policy has the full details.
